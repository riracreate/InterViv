## Difficulty rubric

- **Easy**: high-frequency vocabulary, a single salient grammar rule, a
  connector with no competing reading, or a literal fact stated in the
  passage.
- **Medium**: mid-frequency vocabulary or collocations, parallelism and
  tense-sequence rules, two-blank completion, pronoun/temporal reference
  chains, or passage statements requiring one small inference step.
- **Hard**: low-frequency vocabulary ("laconic", "cogent"), the dangling
  modifier, or a conclusion synthesized from several passage facts while
  rejecting overstatement traps.

Correct-option positions: a×8, b×7, c×8, d×7.

Conventions: no item turns on British vs American usage. The beehive
passage uses the American spelling "favorite" as flavor text only; no
key depends on it. No NOT/EXCEPT stems.
